Publications

Peer-reviewed papers from my work at Priest Lake Veterinary Hospital in Antioch, Tennessee.

These are the papers I've written or coauthored at Priest Lake, most of them sparked by a case or a question from the clinic. Each title links to the journal article, and if I've written about a paper here, that post is linked too.

2026

Lidocaine-ketamine-propofol total intravenous anesthesia in two chinchillas (Chinchilla lanigera)

Kopp L. Veterinary Anaesthesia and Analgesia. 2026;53(6):101371. DOI: 10.1016/j.vaa.2026.101371

Two chinchillas were kept under anesthesia with an intravenous infusion of lidocaine, ketamine, and propofol for dental work, and one also had an ovariohysterectomy. Both kept breathing on their own and recovered without problems, though slow breathing, mild carbon dioxide retention, and low body temperature did occur. The first chinchilla's uterus contained an adenocarcinoma, which my literature review found to be the first reported case of this tumor in a chinchilla.

On this site: Lidocaine-Ketamine-Propofol TIVA in Chinchillas

Portable blood glucose meters do not reliably detect hypoglycemia in ferrets (Mustela putorius furo) compared with a point-of-care chemistry analyzer

Rollins K, Rishniw M, Donnelly TM, Kopp L. American Journal of Veterinary Research. Published online September 30, 2026:1-8. DOI: 10.2460/ajvr.26.05.0224

In blood samples from 24 ferrets, a veterinary glucometer on its dog or cat setting matched an in-clinic chemistry analyzer more closely than a human glucometer did. None of the meters agreed closely enough to diagnose or manage low blood sugar on their own, so low or unexpected readings should be rechecked on a chemistry analyzer.

On this site: Glucometers and Hypoglycemia in Ferrets: New AJVR Data

2025

Gastric dilatation and volvulus in a 5-year-old intact female Holland lop rabbit

Kopp L. The Canadian Veterinary Journal. 2025;66(11):1180-1183. PMID: 41142972. Free full text: PMC12548677

A rabbit with sudden loss of appetite and severe abdominal pain had a displaced spleen on point-of-care ultrasound, which pointed to a twisted stomach. Surgery confirmed a 180 degree gastric dilatation and volvulus, and she recovered fully after the stomach was untwisted and tacked to the body wall. This is the third reported case of GDV in a rabbit and the first in a Holland Lop.

On this site: GDV in a Holland Lop Rabbit: Case Rounds

Hormonal, ultrasonographic and histopathological evidence of ovarian remnant syndrome in a young desexed ferret (Mustela putorius furo) in the United States

Kopp L, Baravik-Munsell ED, Clary E. Veterinary Record Case Reports. 2025;13(3):e70103. DOI: 10.1002/vrc2.70103

A 5.5-month-old spayed ferret with a swollen vulva improved after an hCG injection, which ruled out adrenal disease. Ultrasound found leftover ovarian tissue near the left kidney, and after it was removed her hormone levels dropped to normal spayed values. She is the youngest ferret reported with ovarian remnant syndrome.

On this site: Ovarian Remnant in a 5.5-Month-Old Ferret: Case Rounds (for veterinarians) and Spayed Ferret Acting Like She's in Heat? (for families)

2024

Rhabdomyosarcoma in a Rabbit

Kopp L, Garner M, Priest K. Topics in Companion Animal Medicine. 2024;61:100892. DOI: 10.1016/j.tcam.2024.100892

An older Holland Lop with sudden lameness had a broken femur caused by a muscle tumor, a pleomorphic rhabdomyosarcoma, which was confirmed with special stains after the leg was amputated. The rabbit lived 396 days after surgery, and a necropsy then showed the tumor had spread to several organs.

Initial sterility study of a rabbit hemorrhagic recombinant subunit vaccine when used in a multidose manner across 16 days

Kopp L, Rajeev S, Jones RD. Journal of Exotic Pet Medicine. 2024;49:23-25. DOI: 10.1053/j.jepm.2024.03.004

Two refrigerated vials of the RHDV2 vaccine, punctured daily with careful aseptic technique, grew no bacteria or fungi over 16 days (21 bacterial and 5 fungal cultures). The study was small and did not include anaerobic cultures or endotoxin testing, so the result is encouraging rather than conclusive.

On this site: Can an Opened RHDV2 Vaccine Vial Stay Sterile? Our 16-Day Multidose Study
