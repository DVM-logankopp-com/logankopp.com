Ferret veterinarian in Nashville

Adrenal disease, insulinoma, and other ferret problems seen at Priest Lake Veterinary Hospital.

Middle Tennessee does not have many clinics that see ferrets often. Dr. Logan Kopp, DVM, DABVP (Exotic Companion Mammal Practice), sees ferrets at Priest Lake Veterinary Hospital, 2445 Morris Gentry Blvd, Nashville, TN 37013. Call (615) 361-4646 to schedule.

Problems ferrets are seen for

  • Adrenal disease, including hair loss, itchy skin, and a swollen vulva
  • Insulinoma and episodes of weakness or staring
  • Vomiting, diarrhea, or a ferret that is suddenly quiet
  • Surgery when medical care is not enough, planned with ferret-specific anesthesia

He is the only veterinarian currently practicing in Tennessee as a Diplomate in Exotic Companion Mammal Practice. Referring clinics in Franklin, Brentwood, Murfreesboro, and Nashville are welcome to send records ahead of the visit. If you are a veterinarian referring a case, here is what to include. A weak or collapsed ferret should be discussed by phone before you put the animal in the car, and after hours those calls go through Veterinary Emergency Group in Franklin, Murfreesboro, or West Nashville.
