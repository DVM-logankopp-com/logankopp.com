About Dr. Kopp

Dr. Logan Kopp is a Nashville native. He earned his Bachelor of Science from Lipscomb University and his Doctor of Veterinary Medicine from the University of Tennessee. He is a Diplomate of the American Board of Veterinary Practitioners (Exotic Companion Mammal Practice) and a Fellow in Companion Animal Pain Management.

He is the only veterinarian currently practicing in Tennessee with that specialty title. His clinical work is based at Priest Lake Veterinary Hospital in Antioch, where he sees rabbits and other exotic companion mammals referred from practices across Middle Tennessee.

Dr. Kopp also consults for the Veterinary Information Network on exotic companion mammal cases. He is the only Gold-level rabbit-friendly veterinarian certified by the Rabbit Welfare Association and Fund in North America, and he is listed with the House Rabbit Society.

He does not treat birds or reptiles.

Diagnostics and Pain

In-house laboratory, digital radiography, ultrasound, and CT through Priest Lake Veterinary Hospital. Pain assessment is part of every case.

Surgery

Procedures offered for exotic companion mammals include rabbit appendectomy, abscess surgery, abdominal surgery for an intestinal obstruction, liver lobectomy for liver lobe torsion, nasal and ear surgery, and dental extractions. Ferrets are seen for adrenal surgery. Rats are seen for mastectomy when a mammary tumor needs to come off. Spay and neuter, mass removal, and exploratory surgery are also offered, depending on the species and the size of the patient. CT is arranged with Nashville Veterinary Specialists when a case needs it.

All care is provided at Priest Lake Veterinary Hospital in Antioch (Nashville), Tennessee. Serving exotic pet owners and referring veterinarians throughout Middle Tennessee, including Franklin, Brentwood, Murfreesboro, and surrounding areas.

Questions People Ask

Is there an exotic companion mammal specialist in Nashville?

Dr. Logan Kopp is a Diplomate of the American Board of Veterinary Practitioners in Exotic Companion Mammal Practice. He is the only veterinarian currently practicing in Tennessee with that specialty title. Appointments are at Priest Lake Veterinary Hospital, 2445 Morris Gentry Blvd, Nashville, TN 37013.

Where can I take a sick rabbit in Nashville?

Call Priest Lake Veterinary Hospital at (615) 361-4646. The hospital is a Gold-level rabbit-friendly practice. Common visits include a rabbit that has stopped eating, dental disease, urinary problems, and RHDV2 vaccination. After hours, exotic companion mammal consults go through Veterinary Emergency Group in Franklin, Murfreesboro, or West Nashville.

Do you see guinea pigs, ferrets, and chinchillas?

Yes. Dr. Kopp\u2019s Exotic Companion Mammal Practice includes rabbits, ferrets, guinea pigs, chinchillas, hedgehogs, sugar gliders, rats, mice, degus, and other companion mammals. He does not treat birds or reptiles.

Can my veterinarian refer a case?

Yes. Referring veterinarians can call (615) 361-4646 or use the Priest Lake referral form. Records, recent weights, and any radiographs are useful. Patients return to the primary clinic for ongoing care.
